# User guide

Sign in with your college email → enter the 6-digit code (valid 5 minutes). Use **Resend** after the countdown.

## Student
* **Dashboard** – team, title status, the *Review timeline* (Review 1 → 2 → 3 → Final report), **Review stages** (how many reviews your Guide approved) and **Project milestones** (what your team reported). These are different measures and are never combined.
* **My project** – one tab per review.
  * *Team leader*: fill the form and **Submit**. After “Changes required”, edit and submit a new version; earlier versions and feedback stay on record.
  * *Review 2*: the leader reports every milestone (Not started / In progress / Completed, optional %). **Every member** (leader included) can use **Record my contribution**. Contributions show *Pending review* until your Guide acknowledges them – they are self-reported, not verified.
  * *Review 3*: new features, improvements, testing status, issues, pending work, optional document; compare with Review 2 below the form.
  * *Final report*: upload a PDF (size limit shown on the form). A report approved by your Guide is final.
* **Review slots** – only if booking is on. Pick an available slot (confirm dialog). You can hold one slot per review session; you may cancel while it is pending. If booking is off you can still submit progress and reports.

## Guide
* **Review queue** – submissions and contributions waiting for you, and teams that must correct something.
* **Team page → Record decision** – *Under review*, *Approve* or *Request changes* (feedback required). Edit a title with **Edit title** (reason required; the change is recorded) and **Confirm title** when needed. Add/remove members of your own teams and set the leader. **To remove the current team leader you must choose the new leader in the same step**; a team is never left without one (a sole-member leader cannot be removed; add another member or archive the team).
* **Slots & bookings** – create your own slots (single or date range, with **Preview slots** first), approve/reject requests, cancel an approved booking (reason required).
* **Reports** – export team-wise/student-wise data for your teams.

## Coordinator (assigned sections only)
* **Teams / People / Imports** – create teams, add students (single or bulk import: template → upload → preview → fix errors → **Confirm**; existing records are never overwritten).
* **Review setup** – *Customise for my section* copies a college review so you can change deadlines, fields, milestones and upload rules for your section only.
* **Slots & bookings** – create slots for guides who have teams in your section, set your section's booking policy (within HOD limits), and **Override…** a booking (reason required; audited).
* **Reports** – Excel/CSV exports for your sections with filters.

## HOD
Everything above for all sections, plus **Sections** (capacity, coordinators), **People** (create staff, change roles, archive/restore), **Settings** (team size, guide workload, section capacity, upload defaults, title reconfirmation, time zone), **Slots & bookings → Policy** (college-wide on/off and rules), and the **Audit log**.

## Good to know
* Long lists (People, Teams, slots, booking requests) show 25–100 rows per page with **Previous / Next**; your search and filters are kept when you change page, and the totals always count every matching record.
* If you are told that sign-in codes cannot be sent, the administrator must check the email settings; no code is issued in that case, so your request limit is not used up.
* A guide can only hold the configured number of teams, and a section only its configured number of students; when the limit is reached the action is refused with a clear message (nothing is reassigned automatically).
* Accounts and teams are **archived**, not deleted, so history is preserved.
* Reviews that already have submissions can be edited only in safe ways (fields/milestones can be *retired*, not removed). Deleting such a review archives it.
* Exports include the generation time and the filters applied, and contain only recorded data (nothing is estimated).
